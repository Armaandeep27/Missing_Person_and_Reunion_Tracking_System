Case modules

Built for the real work behind every recovery

The system keeps operational data structured: who is missing, where sightings were reported, which agency owns follow-up, and what support is needed after reunion.

Missing Cases

Store identity, guardian contact, last-seen details, agency assignment, and case status.

Sighting Reports

Capture public or agency sightings with confidence level, location, and verification status.

Rehabilitation

Track shelter, health, counselling, education, and follow-up support after rescue.

Support Programs

Manage scholarship, medical aid, shelter aid, sponsor records, and program status.
